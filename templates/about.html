Our Mission & Standards

About RAA Earthquake

An independent technology platform dedicated to real-time seismic monitoring, multi-hazard risk awareness, and research-backed public safety education.

RAA Earthquake is an independent platform developed by Rishav Bharadwaj and Anurag Raj with a focused objective: to make complex, life-critical seismic data accessible, visually intuitive, and educationally valuable for students, researchers, families, and communities globally.

Our Core Purpose: Democratizing access to open-science geophysical telemetry, promoting research-backed life safety habits (such as Drop, Cover, and Hold On), and building comprehensive disaster literacy without sensationalism or alarmism.

Why We Built RAA Earthquake

Earthquakes occur continuously along our planet's tectonic boundaries. While global agencies like the USGS, EMSC, and national meteorological departments publish robust raw telemetry, accessing and understanding this data in real time can be intimidating for non-specialists.

RAA Earthquake bridges this gap by combining automated data pipelines with clear visual mapping, depth profiles, state-level hazard indices for India, and comprehensive disaster survival guides. We strive to provide a single, dependable platform where users can verify seismic events and immediately learn how to protect themselves.

📡

Open Scientific Telemetry

Direct automated ingestion of GeoJSON feeds from the USGS Earthquake Hazards Program, providing accurate Moment Magnitudes, hypocenter depths, and coordinates.

🛡️

Evidence-Based Safety

Safety guidelines, preparedness checklists, and 72-hour survival kit guides compiled from official standards (NDMA India, FEMA, and International Red Cross).

📖

Educational Integrity

Deep educational articles explaining fault mechanics, seismograph physics, plate tectonics, and historical disasters written in clear, accessible language.

Editorial & Scientific Standards

We maintain strict editorial principles across all content published on RAA Earthquake:

  • No Sensationalism or Clickbait: We report seismic magnitude and depth accurately according to official scientific measurements without exaggerated headlines.
  • Rejection of False Predictions: In alignment with global seismological consensus, we do not entertain or publish unscientific claims of precise short-term earthquake predictions.
  • Clear Attribution: All live feeds cite primary sources, including USGS, NDMA, IMD, and EMSC.
  • Regular Reviews: Educational guides and emergency helplines are audited periodically to ensure numbers, links, and safety protocols remain accurate and current.

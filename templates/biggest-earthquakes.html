🌍 Top 10 Strongest Earthquakes in History (Complete List & Facts)

🌊 Why These Earthquakes Were So Dangerous

  • High magnitude releases massive energy
  • Tsunamis increase destruction
  • Population density increases impact
  • Weak infrastructure leads to more damage

🧠 Key Observations

  • Most strong earthquakes occur near tectonic plate boundaries
  • Pacific Ring of Fire is highly active
  • Undersea earthquakes can trigger tsunamis

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some of the most common inquiries we receive.

Where does your earthquake data come from?
Our real-time earthquake data is sourced directly from the United States Geological Survey (USGS) Earthquake Hazards Program. We receive updates within minutes of seismic detection.
How often is the live map updated?
The live map updates automatically every few minutes to ensure you're seeing the most recent seismic activity. You can also manually refresh the page to fetch the latest data instantly.
Is RAA Earthquake free to use?
Yes, our platform is completely free for everyone. Our mission is to spread awareness and education about earthquake safety, which we believe should be accessible to all.
Can I contribute articles to your platform?
We are always open to collaboration with educational researchers or enthusiasts. Please reach out via the contact form above with your proposal.

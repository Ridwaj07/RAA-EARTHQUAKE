Real-time earthquake data displayed on RAA Earthquake is sourced via public API feeds provided by the United States Geological Survey (USGS) Earthquake Hazards Program, with supplementary data from international seismological networks. Please note that:

  • Initial earthquake determinations (magnitude, epicenter, and depth) are calculated automatically by computer algorithms and are subject to subsequent revision by professional seismologists.

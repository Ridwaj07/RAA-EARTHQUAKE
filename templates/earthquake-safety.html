🛑 Earthquake Safety Tips: What to Do Before, During & After (Complete Guide)

Earthquakes can happen suddenly and without warning, making them one of the most dangerous natural disasters. While we cannot prevent earthquakes, we can reduce their impact by being prepared and knowing what to do at the right time.

This complete guide will help you understand how to stay safe before, during, and after an earthquake. These safety tips can save lives and reduce injuries during such emergencies.

🌍 Why Earthquake Safety is Important

Every year, thousands of people are affected by earthquakes around the world.

  • Reduces panic during emergencies
  • Helps you react quickly
  • Protects your family and property
  • Increases survival chances

⚠️ BEFORE AN EARTHQUAKE

🏠 Secure Your Home

  • Fix heavy furniture
  • Secure shelves
  • Keep heavy items low
  • Install latches

🎒 Emergency Kit

  • Water & food
  • First aid kit
  • Flashlight
  • Power bank

📍 Safe Spots

  • Under table
  • Interior wall
  • Away from glass

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Plan

  • Meeting point
  • Emergency contacts
  • Practice drills

🚨 DURING AN EARTHQUAKE

🛑 Drop, Cover, Hold

Most important safety rule

🏠 Indoors

  • Stay inside
  • Avoid windows
  • No elevators

🏢 Building

  • Stay where you are
  • Protect head

🚗 Driving

  • Stop safely
  • Stay in vehicle

⏱ AFTER AN EARTHQUAKE

🩺 Check Injuries

Help others & give first aid

🔥 Hazards

Check gas, fire, electricity

🏚️ Buildings

Avoid damaged areas

📢 Follow Info

Listen to authorities

🌊 Special Case: Tsunami Risk

After a strong earthquake in coastal areas:

  • Move to higher ground
  • Do not wait for warning

🧠 Common Mistakes

  • Running during shaking
  • Using elevators
  • Standing near glass
  • Ignoring aftershocks

🏫 Importance of Drills

Practice helps react faster in real situations.

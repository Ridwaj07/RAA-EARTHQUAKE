Official Disaster Management & Seismology Resources

A curated reference index of verified national disaster management authorities, geological surveys, and emergency response portals worldwide.

Emergency Note: If you are experiencing an immediate disaster or life-threatening situation, call your country's national emergency number immediately (112 in India & Europe, 911 in the USA/Canada, 999 in the UK).

🇮🇳 India National Disaster & Meteorological Agencies

NDMA — National Disaster Management Authority

Apex statutory body headed by the Prime Minister of India, responsible for framing policies, laying down guidelines, and coordinating disaster response.

📞 Helpline: 011-26701728 / 1070
Visit ndma.gov.in →

IMD — India Meteorological Department

National meteorological service responsible for seismic monitoring via the National Center for Seismology (NCS), cyclone warnings, and severe weather forecasting.

🌐 Seismology Portal: seismo.gov.in
Visit imd.gov.in →

NDRF — National Disaster Response Force

Specialized multi-disciplinary force trained for rapid disaster response, search and rescue operations during earthquakes, floods, and landslides.

📞 24/7 Control Room: 011-24363260 / 9711077372
Visit ndrf.gov.in →

GSI — Geological Survey of India

Premier scientific agency conducting geo-hazard mapping, landslide zonation, active fault mapping, and post-earthquake geological assessments across India.

🌐 Portal: gsi.gov.in
Visit gsi.gov.in →

🌍 International Seismological & Disaster Centers

USGS — Earthquake Hazards Program

United States Geological Survey's global monitoring division, delivering open seismic data, Moment Magnitude calculations, and ShakeMap shake intensity modeling.

Visit earthquake.usgs.gov →

EMSC — Euro-Mediterranean Seismological Centre

European-Mediterranean rapid real-time earthquake detection network with citizen seismology and "Did You Feel It" crowd-sourced ground shaking reports.

Visit emsc-csem.org →

JMA — Japan Meteorological Agency

World-leading agency in earthquake early warning (EEW) systems, seismic intensity scale (Shindo), and Pacific tsunami warning services.

Visit jma.go.jp →

NOAA — Pacific Tsunami Warning Center (PTWC)

International tsunami warning center monitoring undersea earthquakes and ocean buoy telemetry to issue tsunami alerts across the Pacific and Indian Oceans.

Visit tsunami.gov →

📋 4 Pillars of Household Disaster Preparedness

01. Stay Informed

Know the disaster risks in your area. Subscribe to official early warnings from IMD, NDMA, and local authorities.

02. Family Emergency Plan

Agree on meeting locations, out-of-state emergency contacts, and evacuation routes for all family members.

03. Prepare Go-Bag

Assemble a portable survival kit with 72 hours of water, non-perishable food, first aid, torch, and medicines.

04. Secure Home

Anchor heavy furniture, identify safe spots (under heavy tables), and know how to shut off gas/electricity mains.

🎒 72-Hour Survival Readiness

Emergency Survival Kit (Go-Bag) Guide

In a major disaster, emergency services may take up to 72 hours to reach you. A well-packed survival bag can keep your family safe and self-sufficient.

💧 1. Water & Hydration

Water (minimum 3 litres per person per day for 3 days)
Water purification tablets or portable filter
ORAL Rehydration Salts (ORS) packets
Collapsible water container or canteen

🥫 2. Non-Perishable Food

Ready-to-eat canned meats, fish, fruits, and vegetables
Energy bars, dry fruits, nuts, and biscuits
Manual can opener
High-calorie emergency food rations

🩹 3. First Aid & Medical

First aid kit (bandages, gauze, antiseptic wipes, tape)
7-day supply of prescription medications
Painkillers, antacids, anti-diarrheal medicines
Thermometer, scissors, tweezers, N95 masks

🔦 4. Tools & Lighting

LED Flashlight / Torch with extra batteries
Battery-powered or hand-crank radio (FM/AM)
Loud emergency whistle (to signal rescuers)
Multi-tool knife or Swiss Army knife
Power bank (charged) & mobile charging cables

📄 5. Important Documents & Cash

Waterproof pouch with Aadhaar, Passport, Insurance
Emergency cash (small denominations)
List of emergency phone numbers on paper
Property deeds and medical history records

🧥 6. Sanitation & Clothing

Hand sanitizer, soap, wet wipes, toilet paper
Warm blanket or thermal foil space blanket
Change of sturdy clothing & rain jacket
Dust masks and heavy-duty work gloves

💡 Essential Maintenance Tips for your Survival Kit

  • Keep your Go-Bag in an easily accessible location near your exit door.
  • Review and update your kit every 6 months — rotate food, water, and expiring medicines.
  • Tailor the kit for family members with special needs, infants, elderly, or pets.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At RAA Earthquake, accessible from https://raa-earthquake2-0.onrender.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by RAA Earthquake and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. You can access all our live earthquake maps, search filters, and educational guides without creating an account.

  • Log Files: Like most standard web servers, RAA Earthquake uses log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is used solely for site maintenance, analyzing trends, and administering the platform.
  • Contact Form Inquiries: If you contact us directly via our contact form or email, we receive your name, email address, and message contents to respond to your inquiry.
  • User Accounts (Optional): If you choose to register an account, we store your username, email address, and an encrypted password.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

RAA Earthquake uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences (such as your chosen Light/Dark theme setting) and to record user-specific information on which pages the visitor accesses to optimize browsing performance.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their advertisements and links that appear on RAA Earthquake, which are sent directly to users' browsers.
  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • You can review Google’s privacy policy regarding advertising at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID: G-Z40QWB9RF1) to monitor aggregate traffic patterns, popular articles, and site performance. Google Analytics collects anonymized interaction data without identifying individual users.

5. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We aim to ensure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, rectification, erasure, restrict processing, and data portability of any personal data stored on our servers.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. RAA Earthquake does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at raa.earthquake.2.0@gmail.com.
